How to set it as your ringtone
  1. On iPhone only: export as WAV (or M4A), tap Share → Save to Files. Make sure the name ends in .wav / .m4a, not .txt. Open GarageBand → Audio Recorder → Tracks view → Loop button → Files → Browse items, and import your file. Trim it to 30 seconds or less.
  2. Tap ▾ → My Songs. Press and hold the project → Share → Ringtone → Export → Use sound as → Standard Ringtone.
  3. With a Mac: export as M4R (Mac only), then drag it onto your iPhone in Finder (Sounds, or the General tab). Then pick it in Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone.
